Bennetts Auctioneers are delighted to present No. 2 Wilfield Park to the market. This is an excellent opportunity to acquire this superb property located in this much sought after and convenient location. Features of the property include gas fired central heating, private off street parking for two cars, beautiful bay windows, attractive spacious rear garden and excellent potential to enhance and extend subject to p.p. Extending to approximately 153.8 sq. m the accommodation briefly comprises a entrance hall, hallway, kitchen/dining room, sun room, living room, study, utility and shower room completing the ground floor accommodation. The first floor comprises three spacious bedrooms and a family bathroom.

Externally, to the front, the property is approached by a gravelled driveway providing generous off street parking. Mature hedging and established planting provide excellent privacy, while the attractive bay fronted façade adds to the property’s distinctive character. A pathway to the side provides convenient access to the rear garden.

To the rear, the property enjoys a beautifully maintained and exceptionally private garden, predominantly laid in lawn and framed by an abundance of mature trees, hedging and established shrubbery. A charming pathway leads through the garden to a secluded seating area, creating an ideal setting for outdoor dining and entertaining. The Sun room enjoys a wonderful outlook over the garden and further enhances the connection between the house and its outdoor space.

This charming, detached family home presents a rare opportunity to acquire a property of considerable character on one of Sandymount’s most desirable roads, combining generous accommodation, wonderful privacy and superb potential to further enhance and extend, subject to the necessary planning permission
